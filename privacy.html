MusicScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

MusicScan turns photographs and PDFs of printed sheet music into editable, playable scores. This policy explains what information MusicScan processes and how it is used.

Information MusicScan Processes

  • Music photos and PDFs you submit: Images or PDF pages you scan are sent to the MusicScan reading server (or a self-hosted server you choose in Settings) for optical music recognition. The server temporarily stores the uploaded page and generated reading files so the app can display, review, and rescan measures.
  • Scores, edits, and Apple Pencil ink: Your score library, edits, and ink annotations are stored locally on your device. MusicScan does not upload your library for cloud sync.
  • Account and page-allowance data: When signed in (for example via Sign in with Apple), MusicScan receives your account identifier and authentication token to authorize scan requests, enforce monthly page allowances, and isolate your scan jobs from other users.
  • Subscription data: MusicScan Pro subscriptions are processed exclusively by Apple through the App Store. MusicScan receives signed Apple StoreKit entitlement status to unlock Pro features and never receives or stores payment card details.
  • Diagnostics and advertising: MusicScan does not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA), does not run third-party advertising trackers, and does not sell personal data. Standard server security and error logs may be retained briefly by the hosting infrastructure.

How Information Is Used

Submitted sheet music files are used strictly to recognize notation, return your editable score, support rescanning and edge adjustments, enforce subscription page limits, and protect the service against abuse. Uploaded job files on the hosted service are subject to automatic deletion within 30 days.

Sharing and Service Providers

Submitted pages are processed only by the MusicScan reading server and its cloud infrastructure providers (such as Modal for optical music recognition and Supabase for authentication and entitlement records). Apple processes App Store purchases. MusicScan does not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Your Choices & Controls

  • You can delete saved scores anytime inside the MusicScan library or by removing the app from your device.
  • You can manage or cancel a MusicScan Pro subscription anytime in your Apple Account Subscriptions settings.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated server records through the Account screen in the app or by contacting support.

Children's Privacy

MusicScan is a general music practice and engraving utility and is not directed to collecting personal information from children under 13. Please do not submit personal information inside photographed sheet music.

Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as MusicScan evolves. The latest version is always published at this URL. For privacy questions or support requests, please reach out via the MusicScan Support & Issues page.
