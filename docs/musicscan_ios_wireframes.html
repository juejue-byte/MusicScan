What Got Cut on iPhone (Zero Features Lost)
  • Killed the bottom Tab Bar inside the score: Tab bars are top-level only (iOS HIG). Hiding it inside the score frees 64pt and hands the thumb zone to the adaptive Play / Fix / Edit dock.
  • Deleted 4 redundant bars & debug telemetry: Removed the floating app icon row, raw timestamp, `16.4s (GPU 8.0s)`, `- 60% +` zoom buttons (pinch-to-zoom is native), and the 3× duplicated `Fix it` badges.
  • Moved secondary actions into the Title Menu (`▾`): Tap `Waltz in C Minor ▾` for Rename, Pages, Adjust Edges, Retake Photo, and Export — standard iOS document pattern (Pages, Notes, GoodNotes).
  • Contextual `Fix It` photo strip: Instead of permanently crowding the screen with `Photo`, `Bar photo`, and `Compare`, tapping **Fix (6)** slides in the cropped original photo *directly above the active bar* with `✓ Looks Right` / `Edit Notes`.
